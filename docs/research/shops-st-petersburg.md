# Shops — St. Petersburg / Weedon Island / St. Pete Pier / Skyway

Research output, **not yet integrated**. Region 2 of 4. Reviewer decides what
reaches `src/data/shops.ts`.

Recovered from a sub-researcher whose parent never compiled it (see
`docs/LESSONS_LEARNED.md`). Recorded verbatim.

**Method note:** the session WebSearch budget (200/200) was exhausted partway
through. The rest was done with direct WebFetch/curl against business sites,
FWC's Tampa Bay Boating & Angling Guide, OSM Overpass, and the US Census
geocoder. Yelp and Facebook 403 to WebFetch, so their dates come from
search-result titles only and are labelled as such.

## Practical takeaways for the guide
- **Weedon Island:** Riviera and Mitch's are the nearest live-bait shops; 4th
  Street Tackle a third. Nearest **trailer** ramp is **Sunlit Cove**, not
  Weedon Island itself — the preserve is kayak/canoe only.
- **St. Pete Pier:** Gator Jim's is literally on the fishing deck; the
  Municipal Marina ship store is a short walk; Demens Landing is the ramp.
- **Strike from any pre-existing list:** Mastry's, Bait Bucket / Tierra Verde,
  Maximo's "Castaways" bait claim, and "Tampa Bay Fisheries".
