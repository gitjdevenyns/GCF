Resources & installation

Add to iPhone Home Screen

Open the hosted app in Safari, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

For full offline/install behavior, serve the folder over HTTPS. Opening index.html directly still works for most features, but iOS will not register the service worker from a local file.

Official rules

FWC Saltwater Regulations: myfwc.com/fishing/saltwater/recreational/

FWC Fish Rules app: location-aware official regulation reference.

Emergency: Wear a PFD while paddling, carry water, watch lightning, and avoid handling large tarpon out of water.
