Dangerous & handle-with-care species

Graphic injury warning: This section intentionally displays real medical injury photographs automatically so anglers understand the consequences of unsafe handling.

Saltwater anglers regularly catch fish they were not targeting. The safest default for an unfamiliar fish is: do not lip it, do not grab around the fins, control the line, use long pliers, and cut the leader if handling becomes unsafe.

Especially important from a kayak: do not bring a large shark, ray or thrashing barracuda into your lap or cockpit. Release alongside whenever possible.

Safety tools that belong in the tackle bag

Long-nose pliers • long dehooker • line/leader cutters • small bolt/cable cutters if targeting sharks • landing net • gloves for line/leader work • first-aid kit • PFD • flashlight/headlamp.
